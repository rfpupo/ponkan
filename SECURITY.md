# Security policy (draft)

Do not post credentials, account identifiers, exploit details, real .pkn exports, cloud preference snapshots, or personal screenshots publicly.
Once the official repository is public, use its private vulnerability reporting feature when enabled. Until then, do not submit reports to unrelated repositories.
Provide affected version, synthetic reproduction, impact and remediation suggestions. Allow time for review and coordinated disclosure.
Ponkan stores sensitive settings locally and uses a logged-in Riot Client session. Authenticode and updater signatures establish provenance, not absence of vulnerabilities.
There are no guaranteed response times or verified support promises in this draft.
